# Mindware Lab

Mindware Lab builds applied cognitive training and readiness products based on the Trident-G theory of adaptive intelligence.

## What this organization is for

This organization is the public home for Mindware Lab's applied work: cognitive training, readiness workflows, research tooling, and product experiments that turn adaptive-intelligence theory into usable systems.

The lab focuses on practical, human-supervised tools that help people train attention, reasoning, resilience, and decision quality without treating assessment or automation as a replacement for judgment.

## Repository scope

Public repositories in this organization may include:

- product prototypes and support tooling
- research notes suitable for public release
- evaluation and readiness methods
- documentation for Trident-G-aligned cognitive-training workflows
- reusable templates for safe, evidence-led product development

Private repositories may contain active implementation work, unpublished research material, operational details, or sensitive project context.

## Principles

Mindware Lab work should be understandable, evidence-led, privacy-conscious, and designed for human oversight. Public material should make clear what a repository does, what stage it is in, and how it relates to the wider Mindware Lab and Trident-G research programme.
